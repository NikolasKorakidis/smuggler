## What Anemos is built on

- The Godot Engine (MIT licence) and its built-in FastNoiseLite (MIT).
- Published techniques, implemented from scratch: A. Schneider, *The
  Real-time Volumetric Cloudscapes of Horizon Zero Dawn* (SIGGRAPH 2015) and
  *Nubis* (2017); S. Hillaire, *Physically Based Sky, Atmosphere and Cloud
  Rendering in Frostbite* (SIGGRAPH 2016); D. D. Vicroy, *A Simple, Analytical,
  Axisymmetric Microburst Model* (NASA TM-104053, 1991); Koschmieder's
  visibility law; N-wave thunder synthesis after Few (1969) and Ribner & Roy
  (1982).
