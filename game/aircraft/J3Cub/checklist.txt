# Tutorial plane (Cub-type): hand-propped, no electrics, no flaps.
#   condition | ITEM | STATE | how to do it | cockpit part to highlight (optional)

== BEFORE START | until:engine_running
parking_brake_on | BRAKES         | SET             | Press . (period) to set the brakes                                     |
fuel:ON          | FUEL VALVE     | ON              | FUEL valve on the panel, right: click it, or press B                   | FuelValve
throttle_closed  | THROTTLE       | CLOSED          | Throttle fully back: hold F, or pull the HOTAS lever back              |
magnetos_off     | SWITCH         | OFF             | "Switch off!" The SWITCH knob on the panel, left, must read OFF        | Magnetos
pulled_through   | PROPELLER      | PULL THROUGH    | Press Enter 3-4 times to pull the prop through and suck in fuel        |

== STARTING ENGINE | until:engine_running
throttle_cracked | THROTTLE       | CRACKED         | Throttle just above idle: tap R                                        |
magnetos_both    | SWITCH         | BOTH            | "Contact!" Click the SWITCH 3 times, or press M 3 times                | Magnetos
engine_running   | PROPELLER      | SWING           | Press Enter to swing the prop. Repeat until it fires                   |

== AFTER START | until:moving
oil_pressure     | OIL PRESSURE   | RISING          | Watch the OIL PSI gauge, right side                                    | OilPressure
idle:1300        | THROTTLE       | ABOUT 1000 RPM  | Settle the tachometer around 1000                                      | Tachometer

== BEFORE TAKEOFF | until:airborne
controls_checked | FLIGHT CONTROLS| FREE & CORRECT  | Stick fully forward, back, left, right; both pedals                    |
runup:1700       | THROTTLE       | 1700 RPM        | Brakes hold you. Add power to 1700 on the tachometer                   | Tachometer
mag_check        | SWITCH         | CHECK L, R      | Shift+M to L, Shift+M to R, then M back to BOTH                        | Magnetos
carb_heat_check  | CARB HEAT      | CHECK           | V on: rpm drops. V off again                                           | CarbHeat
idle:1300        | THROTTLE       | IDLE            | Back to about 1000 rpm                                                 | Tachometer
parking_brake_off| BRAKES         | RELEASE         | Press . (period)                                                       |

== TAKEOFF | until:agl_above:300
lined_up         | LINE UP        | ON THE STRIP    | Point the nose straight down the strip                                 |
throttle_full    | THROTTLE       | FULL            | Full power. It will swing left: keep it straight with right pedal      | Tachometer
kias_above:25    | TAIL UP        | 25 KNOTS        | Ease the stick slightly forward to lift the tail                       | Airspeed
airborne         | LIFT OFF       | CLIMB AT 45 MPH | Let it fly off around 40 mph, climb at about 45 mph                    | Airspeed

== AFTER TAKEOFF | until:near:tower:600
agl_above:500    | CLIMB          | 500 FT          | Keep climbing. The Cub climbs slowly; be patient                       | Altimeter

== FLY TO THE TOWER
near:tower:600   | RADIO TOWER    | REACH IT        | Red-and-white tower {dist:tower} away, at your {clock:tower}. Fly to it |
heading_to:strip:25 | TURN BACK   | TOWARDS STRIP   | Turn round the tower and point at the strip: {dist:strip}, {clock:strip} |

== APPROACH AND LANDING | until:landed
near:strip:2500  | THE STRIP      | HEAD FOR IT     | Strip {dist:strip} away at your {clock:strip}. Descend gently           | Altimeter
carb_heat_on     | CARB HEAT      | ON              | Before reducing power: press V                                         | CarbHeat
landed           | TOUCH DOWN     | ON THE STRIP    | Idle over the strip, hold it off, land on all three wheels at ~40 mph  | Airspeed

== AFTER LANDING | until:parked
carb_heat_off    | CARB HEAT      | COLD            | Press V                                                                | CarbHeat
parked           | TAXI           | TO THE HANGAR   | Hangar {dist:parking} at your {clock:parking}. Stop on the yellow square. Zig-zag: you can't see over the nose |

== SHUTDOWN
parking_brake_on | BRAKES         | SET             | Press . (period)                                                       |
idle:1300        | THROTTLE       | IDLE            | Throttle fully back                                                    | Tachometer
magnetos_off     | SWITCH         | OFF             | Shift+M to OFF: the engine stops                                       | Magnetos
engine_stopped   | ENGINE         | STOPPED         | Wait for the propeller to stop                                         |
fuel:OFF         | FUEL VALVE     | OFF             | Click the FUEL valve, or press B                                       | FuelValve
