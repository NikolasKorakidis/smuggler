# 172-type tutorial: start, take off, fly to the radio tower, come back,
# land, taxi to the hangar, shut down. One item per line:
#   condition | ITEM | STATE | how to do it | cockpit part to highlight (optional)
# "== TITLE | until:<condition>": once that is true, what's left of the
# section (and earlier ones) is marked skipped. Conditions: game/ui/checklist.gd.
# Hints may use {dist:tower} {clock:tower} (also strip, parking) and {alt:1000}
# (altimeter reading for 1000 ft above the field). "target:" is what L looks at.

== BEFORE START | until:engine_running
parking_brake_on | PARKING BRAKE  | SET             | Press . (period), or click the PARK BRAKE knob, lower left                  | ParkingBrake
fuel:BOTH        | FUEL SELECTOR  | BOTH            | Round knob lower right: click it, or press B                               | FuelSelector
mixture_rich     | MIXTURE        | RICH            | Red knob pushed in: click it, or hold X                                    | Mixture
carb_heat_off    | CARB HEAT      | COLD            | White knob pushed in: click it, or press V                                 | CarbHeat
master_on        | MASTER SWITCH  | ON              | Red switch, lower left: click it, or press O                               | Master

== STARTING ENGINE | until:engine_running
primed           | PRIMER         | 3 STROKES       | Click the black PRIMER knob 3 times, or press P 3 times (not needed warm)  | Primer
throttle_cracked | THROTTLE       | OPEN A CRACK    | Throttle just above idle: tap R, or ease the HOTAS lever forward           |
manual           | PROPELLER AREA | CLEAR           | Look outside, then click this line                                         |
magnetos_both    | IGNITION KEY   | BOTH            | Click the IGNITION key 3 times (OFF-R-L-BOTH), or press M 3 times           | Ignition
engine_running   | STARTER        | ENGAGE          | Click AND HOLD the IGNITION key (or hold Enter). Let go when it roars       | Ignition

== AFTER START | until:moving
oil_pressure     | OIL PRESSURE   | IN THE GREEN    | Watch the OIL PSI gauge come up into the green                             | OilPressure
idle:1200        | THROTTLE       | 1000 RPM        | Set about 1000 on the tachometer                                           | Tachometer

== BEFORE TAKEOFF | until:airborne | target:strip
controls_checked | FLIGHT CONTROLS| FREE & CORRECT  | Stick fully forward, back, left, right; both pedals                        |
trim_takeoff     | ELEVATOR TRIM  | TAKEOFF         | [ / ] until trim is near neutral                                           |
altimeter_set    | ALTIMETER      | SET             | Mouse wheel over the small knob at the altimeter's lower left until it reads field elevation, {field} | AltimeterKnob
runup:1800       | THROTTLE       | 1800 RPM        | The parking brake holds you. Add power to 1800 on the tachometer           | Tachometer
mag_check        | MAGNETOS       | CHECK L, R      | Shift+M to L, Shift+M to R, then M back to BOTH (watch the rpm drop)       | Ignition
carb_heat_check  | CARB HEAT      | CHECK           | V on: rpm drops. V off again                                               | CarbHeat
idle:1200        | THROTTLE       | IDLE            | Back to about 1000 rpm                                                     | Tachometer
flaps_set:1      | FLAPS          | 10°             | Click the FLAPS switch once, or press G once                               | FlapSwitch
parking_brake_off| PARKING BRAKE  | RELEASE         | Press . (period) or click the PARK BRAKE knob                              | ParkingBrake

== TAKEOFF | until:above_field:300 | target:strip
lined_up         | LINE UP        | ON THE STRIP    | Point the nose straight down the strip                                     |
throttle_full    | THROTTLE       | FULL            | Smoothly full power. Keep it straight with the pedals                      | Tachometer
kias_above:55    | ROTATE         | 55 KNOTS        | Watch the airspeed. At 55 kt ease the stick back                           | Airspeed
airborne         | LIFT OFF       | CLIMB AT 65 KT  | Let it fly off, then hold about 65 knots                                   | Airspeed

== AFTER TAKEOFF | until:near:tower:600 | target:tower
above_field:300  | CLIMB          | 300 FT          | 300 ft above the field: altimeter {alt:300}. Climb at 65-70 knots          | Altimeter
flaps_set:0      | FLAPS          | UP              | Click the FLAPS switch with Shift, or press T                              | FlapSwitch
above_field:1000 | CLIMB          | 1000 FT         | 1000 ft above the field: altimeter {alt:1000}. Then level off, ~2300 rpm. Tower {dist:tower} at {clock:tower} (hold L to look) | Altimeter

== FLY TO THE TOWER | target:tower
near:tower:600   | RADIO TOWER    | REACH IT        | Red-and-white tower {dist:tower} away, at your {clock:tower}. Hold L to look at it |
heading_to:strip:25 | TURN BACK   | TOWARDS STRIP   | Turn round the tower and point at the strip: {dist:strip}, {clock:strip}   | Heading

== APPROACH AND LANDING | until:landed | target:strip
near:strip:2500  | THE STRIP      | HEAD FOR IT     | Strip {dist:strip} away at your {clock:strip} (hold L). Descend to 500 ft above it: altimeter {alt:500} | Altimeter
carb_heat_on     | CARB HEAT      | ON              | Before reducing power: press V                                             | CarbHeat
flaps_at_least:2 | FLAPS          | 20° OR MORE     | Below 85 knots: press G twice. Aim for 65 knots on final                   | FlapSwitch
landed           | TOUCH DOWN     | ON THE STRIP    | Power to idle over the strip, flare gently, hold the nose up               | Airspeed

== AFTER LANDING | until:parked | target:parking
flaps_set:0      | FLAPS          | UP              | Shift+click the FLAPS switch or press T until up                           | FlapSwitch
carb_heat_off    | CARB HEAT      | COLD            | Press V                                                                    | CarbHeat
parked           | TAXI           | TO THE HANGAR   | Hangar {dist:parking} at your {clock:parking} (hold L). Stop on the yellow square |

== SHUTDOWN
parking_brake_on | PARKING BRAKE  | SET             | Press . (period)                                                           | ParkingBrake
idle:1100        | THROTTLE       | IDLE            | Throttle fully back                                                        | Tachometer
mixture_cutoff   | MIXTURE        | CUT-OFF         | Click the red MIXTURE knob (pulls it out), or hold Z                       | Mixture
engine_stopped   | ENGINE         | STOPPED         | Wait for the propeller to stop                                             |
magnetos_off     | IGNITION KEY   | OFF             | Shift+click the key, or Shift+M, back to OFF                               | Ignition
master_off       | MASTER SWITCH  | OFF             | Click the red MASTER switch, or press O                                    | Master
