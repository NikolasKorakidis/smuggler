# 172-type checklist. One item per line:
#   condition | ITEM | STATE | how to do it | cockpit part to highlight (optional)
# "== TITLE" starts a section. Items tick in order once their condition is met.
# Conditions are defined in game/ui/checklist.gd; "manual" ticks when clicked.

== BEFORE START
parking_brake_on | PARKING BRAKE  | SET             | Press . (period), or click the PARK BRAKE knob, lower left                 | ParkingBrake
fuel:BOTH        | FUEL SELECTOR  | BOTH            | Round knob lower right: click it, or press B                              | FuelSelector
mixture_rich     | MIXTURE        | RICH            | Red knob pushed in: click it, or hold X                                   | Mixture
carb_heat_off    | CARB HEAT      | COLD            | White knob pushed in: click it, or press V                                | CarbHeat
master_on        | MASTER SWITCH  | ON              | Red switch, lower left: click it, or press O                              | Master

== STARTING ENGINE
primed           | PRIMER         | 3 STROKES       | Click the black PRIMER knob 3 times, or press P 3 times (not needed warm) | Primer
throttle_cracked | THROTTLE       | OPEN A CRACK    | Throttle just above idle: tap R, or ease the HOTAS lever forward          |
manual           | PROPELLER AREA | CLEAR           | Look outside, then click this line                                        |
magnetos_both    | IGNITION KEY   | BOTH            | Click the IGNITION key 3 times (OFF-R-L-BOTH), or press M 3 times          | Ignition
engine_running   | STARTER        | ENGAGE          | Click AND HOLD the IGNITION key (or hold Enter). Let go when it roars      | Ignition
oil_pressure     | OIL PRESSURE   | IN THE GREEN    | Watch the OIL PSI gauge come up into the green                            | OilPressure
idle:1200        | THROTTLE       | 1000 RPM        | Set about 1000 on the tachometer                                          | Tachometer

== BEFORE TAKEOFF
controls_checked | FLIGHT CONTROLS| FREE & CORRECT  | Stick fully forward, back, left, right; both pedals                       |
flaps:1          | FLAPS          | 0-10°           | G / T, one notch per press                                                |
trim_takeoff     | ELEVATOR TRIM  | TAKEOFF         | [ / ] until trim is near neutral                                          |
runup:1800       | THROTTLE       | 1800 RPM        | Parking brake holds you. Add power to 1800 on the tachometer              | Tachometer
mag_check        | MAGNETOS       | CHECK L, R      | Shift+M to L, Shift+M to R, then M back to BOTH (watch the rpm drop)      | Ignition
carb_heat_check  | CARB HEAT      | CHECK           | V on: rpm drops. V off again                                              | CarbHeat
idle:1200        | THROTTLE       | IDLE            | Back to about 1000 rpm                                                    | Tachometer
parking_brake_off| PARKING BRAKE  | RELEASE         | Press . (period) or click the PARK BRAKE knob. Ready to roll               | ParkingBrake
